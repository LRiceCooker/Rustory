# The Goblin King

## Description
A hulking goblinoid standing nearly seven feet tall, the Goblin King wears a crown of rusted iron and wields a jagged greatsword. His skin is a mottled green-gray, scarred from countless battles. His eyes gleam with cunning intelligence unusual for his kind.

## Backstory
Once a common goblin warrior, he united the fractured goblin clans of the Stonefang Mountains through a combination of brute force and surprising tactical brilliance. He rules from a throne carved into the heart of the mountain, commanding a horde of several hundred goblins, worgs, and hobgoblin lieutenants.

## Personality
- Cruel but pragmatic — will negotiate if outmatched
- Deeply superstitious — fears divine magic
- Protective of his horde — sees them as extensions of his power
- Respects strength — may offer alliance to powerful adventurers

## Tactics
In combat, the Goblin King fights aggressively but retreats if reduced below half HP. He always keeps two hobgoblin bodyguards nearby and uses goblin minions as a screen. He prefers to fight in tight tunnels where his size advantage is maximized.

## Hooks
- He possesses a stolen artifact that the party needs
- A rival goblin chieftain wants him overthrown
- He has captured villagers and demands ransom
