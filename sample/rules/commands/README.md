# Custom Commands

## smite

Thorin attacks the Goblin King with a divine smite. Rolls 1d20 + strength modifier for the attack, compares against the target's AC. On a hit, deals 2d8 radiant damage.

## heal

Restore hit points to Thorin by spending a spell slot. Rolls 1d8 healing and applies it. Requires at least one spell slot remaining.

## perception

Make a Wisdom-based perception check (DC 15) for Thorin. On success or critical, tags the character as perceptive. Uses the ability_check rule from system.toml.
